## Fact fixes found while verifying (applied below; say if you disagree)
1. **Caron at Surat: 1668, not 1667.** The expedition left in 1667 and set up the Surat factory in 1668 (Wikipedia "French India"). The line now reads "In sixteen sixty-eight … and a year later … Marcara" (1669 is confirmed).
2. **Reorganisation: "in the seventeen-twenties", not "in seventeen twenty".** Louis XIV's company was merged into Law's company in 1719, Law's company went into receivership in 1721, and the company was rebuilt in 1723 (Wikipedia "French East India Company"). The seal reads "1720s". The line says "rebuilt" instead of "reorganised".
3. Everything else on the verify list checks out: 50-year monopoly (encyclopedia.com / search), Masulipatnam 1669, the 1673 site, the Dutch capture in 1693 with the return after Ryswick, Adyar on 24 Oct 1746 (~350 + ~700 vs ~10,000), Ambur on 3 Aug 1749 where Anwaruddin was killed, Muzaffar Jang as the Nizam's grandson, Arcot (200 Europeans + 300 sepoys, 23 Sep-14 Nov 1751, about 52 days, led by Raza Sahib), Chanda Sahib's death on 12 Jun 1752, Dupleix recalled in 1754 (Godeheu's treaty), Wandiwash on 22 Jan 1760, the Pondicherry surrender in Jan 1761 (16th or 22nd), Lally executed on 9 May 1766, Chandernagore de facto on 2 May 1950, the Kizhur vote on 18 Oct 1954, and Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam all de facto on 1 Nov 1954.
4. Not independently confirmed, kept as the book says: the name Sher Khan Lodi (Wikipedia says "the qiladar of Valikondapuram", which fits the book), Dupleix as "governor south of the Krishna" (Wikipedia only says Muzaffar Jang granted him "territories and titles", 31 Dec 1750), Bussy's army paid by the Nizam, and Clive's line via Lyall (shown as a labelled paraphrase).
