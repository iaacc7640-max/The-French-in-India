## Description
How did France beat an army ten times its size, and still lose India? In 1746, about a thousand French-led soldiers beat an army of ten thousand in southern India. Within a few years, a French governor was choosing who sat on India's thrones. Yet in 1766 France executed its own general, the Comte de Lally, for losing it all. This animated history explains why: not a battle, but a supply line.
